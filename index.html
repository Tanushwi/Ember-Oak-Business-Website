02 — The Roastery

Small batch. Big obsession.

We started Ember & Oak in 2019 out of a one-room flat in Bandra, with a secondhand 3kg roaster and a stubborn belief that Mumbai deserved better coffee — not the kind that hides behind foam, but the kind that tastes like the farm it came from.

Five years and fourteen thousand bags later, we still roast every Tuesday, still cup every batch, and still ship to your door within 48 hours of roast. Some things shouldn't scale.

03 — Bean to Cup

How we do it.

Four steps, zero shortcuts. Every bag is traceable to the farm and the roast date.

01

Source

We buy directly from eight farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, India, Brazil and Kenya — paying above fair-trade floor every time.

02

Roast

12kg batches, roasted every Tuesday morning. Each batch is logged with a full temperature curve — just like the one you see above.

03

Cup

Every roast is cupped by our Q-grader within 24 hours. If it doesn't score 84+, it doesn't ship. Simple.

04

Ship

Bags are sealed within 48 hours of roast and dispatched across India. Same-day delivery in Mumbai, 2–3 days everywhere else.
